## Part 3 — Submenu and its function

### 1. Dashboard
- **Overview** — Home after login.  
- **Summary** — Short numbers view.  
- **Notes** — Personal / office notes.  

### 2. Approvals
- **Overview** — Approvals summary.  
- **Staff** — Approve or reject staff applications.  
- **Suppliers** — Approve or reject supplier applications.  

### 3. Contacts
- **Overview** — Contacts summary.  
- **Customers** — Create/view buyers. Local POS and online both use these records.  
- **Suppliers** — Who you buy stock from.  
- **Carriers** — Transport companies.  
- **Message Templates** — Ready message templates.  

### 4. Catalog
- **Overview** — Catalog summary.  
- **Products** — Product, price, stock, images.  
- **Product Reviews** — Manage customer reviews.  
- **Categories** — Product categories.  
- **Brands** — Brand names.  
- **Packages** — Package offers.  
- **Units** — kg, litre, piece, etc.  
- **Packing Units** — Packing units.  
- **Attributes** — Extra fields (size, etc.).  
- **Product Labels** — Label printing.  
- **Controls** — Catalog control settings.  

### 5. Purchasing
- **Overview** — Purchasing summary.  
- **Purchase Planning** — What to buy.  
- **Purchase Quotations** — Supplier rates.  
- **Purchases** — Purchase bills; stock increases.  
- **Purchase Payment Summary** — Paid / unpaid to suppliers.  
- **Purchase Re-Orders** — Reorder low stock.  
- **Price Tags** — Price tags.  
- **Rate List** — View/print rate list.  
- **Price Update** — Update rates.  

### 6. Sales
- **Overview** — Sales summary.  
- **Sales & POS** — Local counter sale / invoice.  
- **Sale Quotations** — Manual sale quotations.  
- **Online Quotations** — Web orders before payment; verify, reject receipt, or cancel.  
- **Cancelled Quotations** — Cancelled online quotes (numbers kept).  
- **Online Orders in Queue** — Paid/invoiced orders ready to dispatch.  
- **Forwarded** — Sent to supplier or staff.  
- **Sale Returns** — Returns.  
- **Staff Inbox** — Internal staff messages.  

**Remember:** Reject = receipt only. Cancel = quotation closed → Cancelled list.  

### 7. Store
- **Overview** — Store summary.  
- **Top Bar** — Top strip on the website.  
- **Ticker** — Scrolling notice.  
- **Header** — Logo / phone.  
- **Menu** — Website menu.  
- **Hero Slider** — Home large images.  
- **Pages** — Inner page content.  
- **Footer** — Footer links.  
- **Items** — Store items control.  
- **Agricart Inbox** — Customer messages / notifications.  
- **Reviews** — Product / customer reviews.  
- **Brand Reviews** — Brand reviews.  
- **Testimonials** — Testimonials.  
- **Blog** — Blog posts.  
- **Visitor Analytics** — Visitor stats.  
- **SEO** — Search settings.  
- **About** — About page.  
- **Registration Notes** — Registration instructions.  

### 8. Logistics
- **Overview** — Logistics summary.  
- **Delivery Methods** — COD, advance, etc.  
- **Pending** — Waiting to ship.  
- **Shipped** — In transit.  
- **Delivered** — Delivered.  

### 9. Service
- **Overview** — Service module entry / summary.  

### 10. Reports
- **Overview** — Reports summary.  
- **Stock** — Remaining stock.  
- **Customer Ledger** — Customer account.  
- **Supplier Ledger** — Supplier account.  
- **Sales** — Sales summary.  
- **P&L** — Profit and loss.  
- **Expenses** — Expense report.  
- **Cash** — Cash report.  
- **Season Sales** — Seasonal sales.  
- **Season Purchases** — Seasonal purchases.  

### 11. Staff
- **Overview** — Staff summary.  
- **Staff** — Employee records.  
- **Pay** — Pay settings.  
- **Attendance** — Attendance.  
- **Advances** — Salary advances.  
- **Payroll** — Run payroll.  
- **Leave** — Leave management.  

### 12. Finance
- **Overview** — Finance summary.  
- **Banks** — Bank accounts.  
- **Fund Transfers** — Cash ↔ bank moves.  
- **Categories** — Expense categories.  
- **Expenses** — Record expenses.  
- **Payments** — Pay expenses.  
- **Cash Book** — Cash book.  

### 13. Marketplace
- **Overview** — Marketplace summary.  
- **Sales Channels** — Online / offline channels.  
- **Price Channel** — Channel prices.  
- **Quantity Channel** — Channel quantity rules.  
- **Secret Coding Channel** — Secret coding.  

### 14. Settings
- **Overview** — Settings summary.  
- **Profile** — Shop name, address, logo.  
- **Tax** — Tax settings.  
- **Media** — Images / files.  
- **Product Mark** — Product watermark.  
- **Brand Mark** — Brand watermark.  
- **AI** — AI settings.  
- **AI Logs** — AI usage logs.  
- **Print** — Print profiles.  
- **Access** — Roles and permissions.  
- **Errors** — System errors.  
- **Activity** — Who did what.  
- **Users** — Login users.  
- **Backups** — Data backups.  
- **Reviews** — Brand review message settings.  

### 15. Docs
- **Overview** — Docs introduction.  
- **Full App Guide** — This full-app simple document (Urdu / English).  

### 16. External Apps
- **Overview** — External tools summary.  
- **Re Order** — Personal reorder tool (separate from Purchasing Re-Orders).  
- **Purchase Summary** — Personal purchase summary sheets.

- **Bill Printing** — Bill print.  
- **Barcode Printing** — Barcode print.  
- **Home Expenses** — Home expenses.

- **Economic Radar** — Economic calendar / radar.  
- **Market Signals** — Market signals.  
- **Trade Terminal** — Demo trading.  
- **Account Types** — Account types / rules.  
- **Strategies** — Strategies.  
- **Learning Course** — Learning course.  
- **Account History** — Account history.  

### 18. Personal Vault
- **My Box** — Your private files / passwords.  
- **All Boxes** — Super Admin only: all staff boxes.

## Short flow (memory aid)

Local sale: Contacts → Customers → Sales → Sales & POS  

Online sale: Website order → Online Quotations → (Cancel → Cancelled) or Invoice → Online Orders in Queue → Logistics  

Purchasing: Plan / quotation → Purchase bill → stock up → Payment Summary
